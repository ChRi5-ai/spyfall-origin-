How to Play

A quick guide to Spyfall

1

The Setup

The host creates a room and shares the 4-letter code. Everyone joins, the host starts the game. One or more players are secretly assigned as SPY — the rest are CIVILIANS who know the secret location.

🕵️
SPY
No location given
VS
🏛️
CIVILIAN
Knows the secret location
2

The Discussion

Players take turns asking each other yes or no questions. Civilians try to expose the spy; the spy tries to blend in and guess the location.

Chat Log
Alex → Jordan Have you ever been to this place before? Jordan: YES
Jordan → Sam Is this place usually crowded? Sam: NO
3

Selecting Who to Question

On your turn, click a player in the panel to select them. Your row glows red — the selected target glows cyan.

Chris

🔴 Red = player whose turn it is  |  🔵 Cyan = player being questioned

4

Answering

When questioned, you see YES and NO buttons. That's it — no explanations allowed.

Alex → You Have you ever been to this place before?
5

Voting

After 5 minutes (or when majority press ⚡ VOTE NOW), everyone votes. The player with the most votes is ejected.

6

Win Conditions

😇
Civilians Win — spy is ejected and fails to guess the location.
🕵️
Spy Wins — an innocent is ejected and the spy correctly guesses the location.
⚖️
Draw — the spy is caught but correctly guesses the location anyway.
